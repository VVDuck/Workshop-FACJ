### Week 4 Objectives

* Understand Amazon VPC networking fundamentals.
* Learn how Internet Gateway and NAT Gateway work.
* Manage EC2 networking resources.
* Configure Subnets and Route Tables.
* Practice deploying EC2 instances inside a custom VPC.
