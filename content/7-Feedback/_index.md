> Participating in the **First Cloud Journey Bootcamp - 2026** has been a valuable experience for me. During the 12-week program, I gained AWS knowledge, completed hands-on labs, and had the opportunity to work on a practical cloud project. I sincerely appreciate the FCAJ team for organizing such a meaningful learning program.

### Overall Evaluation

**1. Working Environment**

The learning environment is friendly, supportive, and professional. Mentors and team members are always willing to help whenever I have questions, making the learning process more enjoyable and effective.

**2. Support from Mentor / Team Admin**

The mentors are dedicated and explain concepts clearly. They encourage participants to think independently and solve problems on their own before providing guidance. The admin team is also very responsive and always supports participants throughout the program.

**3. Relevance of Work to Academic Major**

The learning content is closely related to my major and provides many practical AWS skills that I have not learned at university. It helped me better understand how cloud services are applied in real projects.

**4. Learning & Skill Development Opportunities**

Through this program, I improved both technical knowledge and practical skills, including:

- AWS fundamental services
- AWS CLI
- Cloud architecture
- Teamwork
- Documentation
- Problem-solving

The hands-on labs and project helped me gain valuable real-world experience.

**5. Company Culture & Team Spirit**

The learning community is friendly and collaborative. Everyone is willing to share knowledge and support each other, creating a positive atmosphere throughout the bootcamp.

**6. Internship Policies / Benefits**

The learning materials are well organized, and the roadmap is clear. Having access to practical labs and a real project makes the program much more valuable.

---

### Additional Questions

**What did you find most satisfying during your internship?**

The hands-on labs and the opportunity to build a real AWS project helped me understand cloud services much better.

**What do you think FCAJ should improve for future participants?**

The program is already well organized. In the future, it would be great to have more advanced topics such as Docker, Kubernetes, Terraform, or CI/CD.

**Would you recommend this program to your friends? Why?**

Yes. I would recommend it because it provides a clear learning roadmap, practical labs, dedicated mentors, and a supportive learning community.

---

### Suggestions & Expectations

**Do you have any suggestions to improve the internship experience?**

It would be great to organize more technical sharing sessions or project showcase events where participants can learn from each other.

**Would you like to continue this program in the future?**

Yes. I sincerely hope to have the opportunity to continue my involvement with FCAJ.

**Any other comments (free sharing):**

Thank you to all mentors, admins, and the FCAJ team for organizing this bootcamp. This program has helped me build a stronger foundation in AWS and increased my confidence in pursuing a career in Cloud Computing. I hope FCAJ will continue to grow and support more students in the future.
